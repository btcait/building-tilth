## Hero

**Building the conditions for community agency.**

Research, computational tools, and technical assistance that help communities understand, evaluate, and shape the systems affecting their lives.

Building Tilth is an emerging practice focused on durable technical capacity for community advocacy—not just answers delivered from the outside.

## The idea

Technical support should leave more behind than a report. It should strengthen the knowledge, tools, relationships, and independence of the people whose futures are at stake.

## Three practice areas

1. **Make evidence usable.** Translate complex technical questions into accessible research, analysis, visualizations, and practical decision-making resources.
2. **Build capacity that stays.** Pair technical work with learning, documentation, and reusable workflows so advocates can extend and adapt what they gain.
3. **Strengthen public agency.** Support community organizations in engaging with planning, infrastructure, and institutional processes on better-informed terms.

## Prior experience areas (summaries for CV-ready site)

**Energy municipalization & fair decarbonization** — Supporting efforts around community-oriented energy infrastructure municipalization and equitable decarbonization through technical assistance, research-oriented support, and activities that build local advocacy capacity.

**Community-minded small business development** — Supporting local small business development through technical assistance, computational skill-building, and creative collaboration to explore feasible business pathways informed by environmental and social justice commitments.

These descriptions are provisional and intentionally omit client identities, unconfirmed deliverables, and claimed outcomes. They describe work that predates the formal Building Tilth practice.

## Building Tilth Commitment (working draft)

1. **Community-directed value:** Start from affected communities' questions and priorities, not only the commissioning institution's needs.
2. **Transparent, accessible methods:** Make assumptions, findings, and limitations understandable and shareable wherever agreements and rights permit.
3. **Skills and tools that remain:** Design opportunities for learning, documentation, and continued independent use alongside project deliverables.
4. **Independence and accountability:** Make roles, conflicts, and constraints explicit, and protect communities' freedom to question or disagree.

This is a proposed practice commitment, not an independent certification.
