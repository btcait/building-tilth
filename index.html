THE BUILDING TILTH COMMITMENT / 05

Whoever commissions the work, communities should benefit.

A working set of principles for responsible technical assistance. These commitments are intended to guide project design and accountability, not to claim an independent certification.

01

Community-directed value

Start from affected communities' questions and priorities, not only the commissioning institution's needs.

02

Transparent, accessible methods

Make assumptions, findings, and limitations understandable and shareable wherever agreements and rights permit.

03

Skills and tools that remain

Design opportunities for learning, documentation, and continued independent use alongside project deliverables.

04

Independence and accountability

Make roles, conflicts, and constraints explicit, and protect communities' freedom to question or disagree.
